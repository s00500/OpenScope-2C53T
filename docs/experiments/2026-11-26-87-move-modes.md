# EXP-87: MOVE modes, each owning both axes

- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Unit:** my unit
- **Build:** `guest-coldtrace`, this change (`Nov 26 2026 17:10:08`)
- **Setup:** generator off, both channels on (CH1 range 5 AC, CH2 range 5 DC),
  AUTO, timebase 0x08, cursors off, no mask
- **Status:** CONFIRMED

## 1. Problem

What MOVE gave the arrow keys was hard to follow. Its stages split the axes
("Trig level" kept LEFT/RIGHT on the timebase, "Position" kept UP/DOWN on
volts/div), the stage was only named by a popup, there was no vertical
position at all, and cursors took all four arrows whenever they were on. Every
MOVE and arrow press also went through `DCMD_REDRAW_ALL`, a whole-screen clear.
The user asked for three modes: position (left/right and up/down), volts/div
and timebase, trigger level and position.

## 2. Hypothesis

With one mode per pair of axes (`ui/move_mode.h`), the mode shown as a tag in
the top row, and the trigger level, trigger point, vertical positions and the
mode moved into the compositor's live epoch, MOVE presses and the Trigger and
Position arrows repaint through the live compositor: 0 full repaints. A Scale
press (volts/div) still repaints in full, because the info bar and the
measurement badges change with it. Falsifier: full repaints during the
Trigger/Position presses, or none for the Scale presses (which would mean the
counter cannot see a full repaint).

## 3. Procedure

Change:

- `ui/move_mode.h` (pure, `test-move-mode`, 28 checks with a mutant that
  offers Mask/Cursors always): Scale -> Trigger -> Position -> [Mask] ->
  [Cursors] -> Scale. Trigger: UP/DOWN level, LEFT/RIGHT trigger point.
  Position: UP/DOWN vertical position of the active channel, LEFT/RIGHT
  trigger point. Half a grid division per press (13 rows, 16 columns).
- Cursors became a MOVE mode: SELECT turning them on hands them the arrows,
  MOVE takes the arrows back while they stay on screen.
- Vertical position: `scope_vmap_offset()` in the shared vertical map, set by
  `chan_map_prep()` from `channel_state_t.position`, so the full path, the
  compositor, the trigger level line and the mask bounds move together
  (`test-scope-vmap`: offset moves without rescaling, clamps in the band,
  resets on a new fit). The compositor had a comment saying the position was
  deliberately not applied because the full path ignored it; now neither can.
- Mode tag chip in the top row; the trigger level line is drawn denser in
  Trigger mode (it was, for the old "Trig level" stage).
- `scope_ui_epoch` lost `trigger.level` and `chN.position`; `scope_live_epoch`
  gained them, `trig_x` and the mode. The Trigger/Position keys and MOVE send
  `DCMD_SCOPE_LIVE`; Scale keys still send `DCMD_REDRAW_ALL`.
- Shell `mask` prints `move-mode <name>`.

Instrument: the `display` shell command's full-repaint counter
(`scope draws: full N (redraw_all, draw_scope, scope_live, periodic)`), read
before and after a press sequence sent with `openscope press`, no screenshots
in between (a screenshot pauses RUN, which repaints in full).

**Preconditions verified by readback:**

`captures/move-modes/` (`device-*.png` from my unit, RUN paused by the
capture, so the STOP pill shows).

On the glass (screenshots, weak evidence): the tag reads Scale (grey),
Trigger (trigger colour), Position (CH1 colour); one Position press moved the
CH1 trace and its marker up half a division.

## 6. Blind spots

- The counter sees repaints the display task makes; it cannot see a blink a
  human would notice for another reason (it found none in EXP-85 either).
- With the generator off the trigger level line sits at CH1's band edge
  (the level is outside a quiet record), under the top-row chips, so the
  denser Trigger-mode line was seen only in the emulator.
- Vertical position was checked with a quiet record and in the emulator's
  split view; a large signal moved off the band flattens on its edge
  (host-tested), not looked at on my unit.
- The left-edge ground markers ("<1", "<2") are drawn by the full path only,
  at mid-screen minus the position: they follow the position, but they were
  already unrelated to an autofit trace, and the compositor paints over them.

## 7. Conclusion

- **Established:** MOVE gives the arrows one mode at a time, each owning both
  axes, named on the glass; Trigger and Position presses and MOVE itself draw
  through the compositor (0 full repaints on my unit, control 2).
- **Not established:** whether the user finds the order and the tag clear;
  that is their call on the glass.
- **Follow-up:** the ground markers (draw them from the map, or drop them);
  Scale presses still clear the whole screen, which only the info bar and the
  badges need. **Both done the same day in EXP-88.**
